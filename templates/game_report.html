How at-bats are graded. Half from the result (HR 100, 3B 95, 2B 90, 1B 78, BB 68, HBP 62, sac 60, ROE 50, FC 40, out 22, strikeout 4–8) and half from contact quality (xBA from exit velocity and launch angle) when the ball was put in play, so a hard-hit out grades well above a weak single. Added on top: long at-bats (6+ pitches), RBI, two-strike success. Late and close games (7th inning on, within 2 runs) stretch the grade up or down. Baserunners are not in the data, so base-state leverage is not counted. Pinch hitters and substitutes are inferred from batting order. Beta: grades are a starting point for the coaching conversation.
